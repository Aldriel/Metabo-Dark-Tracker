Metabo-Dark Tracker reads an untargeted LC-MS/MS file (mzML or mzXML)
and annotates each MS/MS spectrum.

A spectrum is a library hit when its precursor and a square-root cosine
agree with the small teaching library. Otherwise a characteristic neutral
loss can assign a chemical family. Spectra that match neither stay in the
dark metabolome. Exact-mass formulas are a bounded C, H, N, O, P, S search,
the first step SIRIUS takes, not SIRIUS itself.

The public databases (PubChem, ClassyFire, GNPS) stay off unless you ask.
The built-in plasma file is simulated. It is not a patient sample.
